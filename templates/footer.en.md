## Resources

- [awesome-seo-geo-skills](https://github.com/UnderleveledBuilder/awesome-seo-geo-skills/blob/main/README.en.md): SEO and GEO skills that help search engines and AI answers find your content
- [awesome-short-drama-skills](https://github.com/UnderleveledBuilder/awesome-short-drama-skills/blob/main/README.en.md): AI short-drama and manju skills, from scripts to storyboards and video prompts
- [awesome-story-skills](https://github.com/UnderleveledBuilder/awesome-story-skills/blob/main/README.en.md): open-source methods for getting LLMs to tell stories, also useful for voice-over scripts and story-driven copy
- [anthropics/skills](https://github.com/anthropics/skills): the official Agent Skills repo with the spec and examples, a good starting point for writing your own skill
- [vercel-labs/skills](https://github.com/vercel-labs/skills): the `npx skills` installer, with the [skills.sh](https://skills.sh) directory

## Contributing
